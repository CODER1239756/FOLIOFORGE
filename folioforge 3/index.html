Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Building and publishing your portfolio costs you nothing. If it helped, you can buy me a coffee to cover the AI and hosting costs.

Who can see my portfolio?

Anyone who has the link. Don't add details you want to keep private, such as a home address or phone number.

Can I change it after publishing?

Make your changes, then publish again. You'll get a new link each time. Use Download HTML if you want to keep a copy of your page.

Where does my information go?

To an AI service that writes your page, and to Netlify, which hosts it. FolioForge doesn't keep a database of your answers.

What if the AI is slow or fails?

Use the quick template. It builds your page instantly in your browser and you can publish it the same way.
